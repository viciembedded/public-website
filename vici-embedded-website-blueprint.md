# Vici Embedded --- Professional Website Blueprint

> **Brand tagline:** Engineering Technology. Building Possibilities.

## 1. Website Purpose

Create a professional corporate website that establishes Vici Embedded
as a technology and engineering company, clearly communicates its
capabilities, builds credibility with prospective clients and partners,
and provides a direct route for enquiries and project discussions.

The website should present Vici Embedded as a company that combines
hardware and software engineering to solve practical problems. It should
communicate both sides of the business:

-   **Products:** Technology products conceived, developed and brought
    to market by Vici Embedded.
-   **Services and contracts:** Custom engineering, software and
    technology development for individuals, startups, businesses and
    organizations.

The brand should feel credible, technically capable, innovative and
practical---not like a generic IT support agency or a company claiming
expertise in areas that are still under development.

## 2. Brand Positioning

### Brand name

**Vici Embedded**

### Tagline

**Engineering Technology. Building Possibilities.**

### Short brand description

Vici Embedded is a technology and engineering company developing
practical solutions across embedded systems, software, cybersecurity and
connected technologies. We build our own products and collaborate with
clients to turn technical ideas into working solutions.

### Core positioning

A multidisciplinary engineering company that brings hardware, software
and intelligent systems together to solve real-world problems.

### Brand values

-   **Engineering discipline:** We approach problems methodically, from
    requirements and design through implementation and testing.
-   **Practical innovation:** We focus on technology that addresses real
    needs.
-   **Quality and reliability:** We aim to build solutions that are
    dependable, maintainable and fit for purpose.
-   **Continuous learning:** We research, experiment and evolve as
    technology advances.
-   **Collaboration:** We work with clients and partners to turn ideas
    into useful outcomes.

### Tone of voice

Use language that is: - Professional and confident, without sounding
exaggerated. - Technical enough to establish credibility, but
understandable to non-specialists. - Clear, purposeful and focused on
outcomes. - Forward-looking, while distinguishing current capabilities
from future areas of exploration.

Avoid unsupported claims such as "industry-leading," "world-class,"
"market-leading," or claims about certifications, years of experience,
completed projects and client results unless Vici Embedded can
substantiate them.

## 3. Recommended Website Structure

Build the first version as a responsive corporate website with the
following pages:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Page                                Purpose
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Home                                Introduce the company, communicate
                                      its value and guide visitors to key
                                      sections.

  About                               Explain who Vici Embedded is, its
                                      mission, vision and engineering
                                      approach.

                                      technology and engineering areas.

  Products                            Showcase products developed by Vici
                                      Embedded. If there are no public
                                      products yet, use a clear "Product
                                      development in progress" state
                                      rather than inventing products.

  Contact                             Give prospective clients and
                                      partners a clear way to make
                                      enquiries.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### Recommended navigation

-   Home
-   About
-   Services
-   Products
-   Contact

Use a prominent **Discuss a Project** button in the desktop navigation.
On smaller screens, place the navigation links and CTA inside an
accessible mobile menu.

Optional future pages: - Individual service detail pages - Individual
product detail pages - Projects / Case Studies - Insights / Journal -
Careers

Do not add empty pages simply to make the website appear larger. Add
these sections when there is real content to publish.

# 5. Visual Identity and Design Direction

## Overall direction

The website should communicate engineering capability, modern technology
and practical innovation. Aim for a clean, premium, technical identity
with enough visual character to make Vici Embedded recognizable.

### Suggested visual style

-   Modern and minimal, with strong typography and clear spacing.
-   Dark technical sections balanced with light content sections.
-   Subtle grid systems, line patterns, circuit-inspired details and
    restrained technical diagrams.
-   High-quality images of real hardware, prototypes, workbenches,
    devices and system interfaces when available.
-   Consistent iconography and simple motion.
-   Clear hierarchy and strong calls to action.

### Suggested colour palette

These are starting recommendations, not established brand colours.
Adjust them to suit the final logo.

  Role                 Suggested colour   Hex
  -------------------- ------------------ -----------
  Primary background   Deep navy          `#0B1220`
  Secondary dark       Charcoal blue      `#121C2D`
  Primary accent       Electric blue      `#2878F0`
  Secondary accent     Cyan               `#25C4D8`
  Light background     Off-white          `#F5F7FA`
  Main text on light   Slate              `#263244`
  Muted text           Cool grey          `#718096`

Use accent colours selectively for buttons, links, diagrams and
highlights. Maintain sufficient contrast for accessibility.

### Typography

Use a modern sans-serif typeface with strong readability.

Possible combinations: - **Headings:** Space Grotesk - **Body:** Inter

Alternative: - **Headings:** Sora - **Body:** Inter

Use a clear type scale, comfortable line height and restrained font
weights. Avoid using too many typefaces.

### Logo usage

-   Use the official Vici Embedded logo consistently in the header,
    footer and browser favicon.
-   Prepare light and dark logo variants if needed.
-   Do not distort, stretch or add effects to the logo.
-   If the final logo is not ready, use a clean text-based wordmark
    temporarily.

### Imagery

Prioritize: - Actual prototypes and circuit boards - Microcontrollers
and embedded devices - Electronics development and testing -
Hardware-software integration - Connected devices and sensors -
Technical diagrams and product renders

Use imagery that supports the content. Avoid random technology stock
images that do not represent the company's actual work.

# 6. Functional and Technical Requirements

## Responsive design

-   Fully responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop.
-   Mobile navigation should be accessible and easy to operate.
-   Avoid horizontal overflow and cramped content.
-   Ensure buttons and form controls are comfortable to use on
    touchscreens.

## Performance

-   Optimize and compress images.
-   Use modern image formats where supported.
-   Lazy-load below-the-fold images.
-   Avoid unnecessary animation libraries and oversized assets.
-   Keep page loading fast, particularly on mobile networks.

## Accessibility

-   Use semantic HTML landmarks and a logical heading hierarchy.
-   Provide meaningful alternative text for informative images.
-   Ensure keyboard navigation works.
-   Make focus states visible.
-   Use sufficient colour contrast.
-   Associate form labels with their inputs.
-   Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status.

## SEO

Recommended homepage metadata:

**SEO title:** Vici Embedded \| Technology and Engineering Solutions

**Meta description:** Vici Embedded designs and develops technology
solutions across embedded systems, software development, cybersecurity
and intelligent connected technologies.

Recommended practices: - Unique page titles and meta descriptions. -
Descriptive URLs, such as `/about`, `/services`, `/products` and
`/contact`. - Open Graph metadata for social sharing. - Descriptive
image filenames and alt text. - XML sitemap and robots.txt. -
Appropriate organization structured data, using only verified company
details. - One clear H1 heading per page.

## Contact form and security

-   Validate inputs on both client and server.
-   Protect the form against spam and abuse.
-   Use HTTPS.
-   Do not expose private keys or credentials in frontend code.
-   Provide a privacy notice explaining how enquiry information is used.
-   Store and process submitted information securely.

## Content management

If the company expects to publish products, projects or insights
regularly, consider a CMS or a simple admin-managed content system. If
the content will change infrequently, a static website may be sufficient
for the first release.

## Important content distinction

The current company description includes established areas of focus
alongside expanding and future directions. The website should preserve
that distinction:

-   Present embedded systems engineering and software development as
    core areas where the company is ready to undertake work, subject to
    its actual delivery capacity.
-   Describe cybersecurity and computer technology solutions accurately
    according to the services the company can currently deliver.
-   Label IoT as an expanding capability if the offering is still
    developing.
-   Label AI and intelligent hardware as a future direction or area of
    exploration until there are concrete offerings.

This distinction helps the brand appear credible and avoids promising
services or capabilities that are not yet ready.

# 10. Final Direction for the Website

The finished website should leave visitors with three clear impressions:

1.  **Vici Embedded is an engineering-led technology company**, not
    simply a general-purpose IT service provider.
2.  **The company builds products as well as client solutions**,
    combining hardware and software where appropriate.
3.  **Vici Embedded is focused on practical innovation**, with a clear
    interest in embedded systems, connected technologies and intelligent
    hardware.

The website should be visually distinctive but restrained, technically
credible without unnecessary jargon, and structured to convert genuine
interest into project enquiries and partnerships.
